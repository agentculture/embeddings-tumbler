Nearest texts

    Nothing typed. Every text is shown, coloured by its topic. Type above, or click a point to search from its text.

    What this page shows

    • The query becomes one more point in the space, drawn as a ring.
    • Its nearest texts are found in the full space, never on the screen. They light up and are joined to the query; the rest dim, or hide with Only results.
    • Reranker re-orders the nearest texts with a second scorer and says how far each one moved.
    • Distance difference writes each true distance on its line and shows how much farther each result is than the nearest one.
    • Clicking any point searches from that text.

    Still open for the spec

    • Who embeds the query. A page may not hold the gateway address or key, so something on the server side must do it.
    • Which reranker is used, and where it runs.
    • What distance difference should mean exactly. Here it is the distance to the query and the gap to the nearest result.
    • Whether the view turns to fit the results by default.
    • How search sits beside Tumble, Lattice and Wander.

    Where the data comes from

    Everything here is made in the page. Nothing is sent anywhere.
